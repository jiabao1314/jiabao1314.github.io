## TRAIL/DR5 and cervical cancer migration

My current project at Université Bourgogne Europe investigates the mechanisms of **TRAIL/DR5 axis-induced cervical cancer migration**, supervised by **Olivier Micheau**.

The experimental work includes cell and plasmid models, flow cytometry, Western blotting, and protein-labeling approaches to study receptor-associated interactions.

## Cervical cancer genomics

I am exploring bioinformatics approaches that combine gene expression, clinical information, DNA methylation, copy-number variation, and single-cell data. My interests include HPV-related biology and enhancer regulation in cervical cancer.

## Cervical cancer imaging

My clinical research interests include multisequence MRI and its relationship with clinical reports and pathological findings in cervical cancer.

## Women's health and population research

I also work on questions in women's health, including premenstrual disorders and the analysis of clinical and population data.
