## About me

I am Jiajia Wang, a researcher at **Université Bourgogne Europe (UBE)**. My current project investigates the role of the **TRAIL/DR5 axis in cervical cancer migration**, under the supervision of **Olivier Micheau**.

My work brings together cell-based experiments and bioinformatics. I am interested in how cancer signaling mechanisms can inform questions in women's health.
